Press enquiries

Boilerplate

Short

Happy Toons Studio is an independent UK studio for 3D animation, browser games, short-form video and creative digital services, working in English, Kurdish (Sorani) and Arabic.

Long

Happy Toons Studio is an independent creative studio run from the United Kingdom, founded by Ayub Azizi. It makes 3D animation and promo video, browser games such as Toonblade, Khalifakan and Block Tower, and short-form video for its YouTube channels StepWise Simple and Coilwise. The studio also offers one-page websites, logos and social graphics, voiceover and subtitles, translation and copywriting, in English, Kurdish (Sorani) and Arabic. Its family content, including custom cartoons and the Happy Town series, sits in a separate Kids & Family section. Production is AI-assisted and every piece is reviewed by a person before release.

What we make

  • 3D animation and video: short animated pieces and promo videos in vertical or wide format.
  • Games: Toonblade, خەلیفەکان (Khalifakan), Block Tower and Sharsten Village in the browser, plus the Gurut demo on itch.io. See Games.
  • Creative services: websites, branding, voiceover, subtitles, translation and copywriting. See Services.
  • YouTube: StepWise Simple (step-by-step phone fixes) and Coilwise (quick science and home-electrics Shorts).
  • Kids & Family: custom cartoons, Happy Town videos and family games, in their own section.

Name and usage

  • Write the name as Happy Toons Studio. "Happy Toons" is fine on second mention.
  • Please don't recolour, stretch or redraw the logo. Use it on a white or light background.
  • Please describe our work as AI-assisted where relevant.
