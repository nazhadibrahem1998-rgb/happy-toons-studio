HOW TO PLAY

You are the night courier for Luma Bay Parcel. Each night is a short run. Finish the drops before the clock hits midnight.

Drive with the arrows or WASD. The wheel returns to center, and the car grips the road until you ask it to slide. Shift or Ctrl is the handbrake. Space is nitro. On a phone, drag the wheel on the left. Brake, Drift, Nitro and Fire sit on the right. Auto-accelerate is on for touch, and you can turn it off in Settings. A controller uses the stick to steer, the south button to go, the east button to drift, and the right trigger to fire.

Follow the pink arrow. Roll through a drop ring and the parcel is handed off. If the trunk is full, deliver one or return to the Parcel depot for the rest.

Gold rings are side contracts. They pay more and spend time. Once you take one, finish it. Amber cans refill the tank. Cyan tokens give minutes back. Chevron pads give a speed kick.

Pass close to other drivers for a near-miss bonus. A hard bump rattles the clock forward four minutes. No one is on foot. The other cars are just traffic. From night 2 the car can mount neon tools: a front laser, a pulse shockwave, an EMP that stalls traffic, and a homing plasma orb. They break barricades and jammer drones. Traffic only spins out or coasts. It is never wrecked, and there is no reward for bothering other drivers.

Between nights, the garage sells engine, handling, nitro, trunk space and paint. After you clear Midnight, Endless Coast unlocks. Progress is saved in this browser.

Happy Toons Studio. Art is drawn in the game. Sound is synthesized here. Nothing is fetched from the network.
