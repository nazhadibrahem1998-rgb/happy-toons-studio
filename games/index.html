Help

Questions and answers

How do I play on a phone or tablet?

Tap Play free. The game opens in your browser. Use the on-screen joystick and buttons. Turning your phone sideways (landscape) gives you more room. On some games you can choose "Add to Home Screen" to play full screen.

How do I play on a computer?

Click Play free. Move with WASD or the arrow keys and use the mouse. Each game shows its own controls on the start screen. Press Esc or P to pause.

Is it really free?

Yes. These games are free to play, with no ads and nothing to buy inside them.

Do I need to sign up?

No. There are no accounts, no chat and no sign-up. Your best score or settings stay on your own device.

Can I buy things?

Nothing in these games costs money. If you ever see something to buy anywhere online, always ask a parent or carer first.

A game is slow or won't start

Close other apps and tabs, then reload the page. Use an up-to-date browser such as Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox. Older phones may run 3D games slowly.

Who can I contact?

Grown-ups can email hello@happytoonsstudio.com. Kids, please ask a parent or carer to write for you.
