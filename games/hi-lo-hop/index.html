How to play

Tap the road you want. Sunny hops there. Grab stars, berries and cookies. Slip past the sleepy clouds. Bump a cloud and it is a soft bonk — the round ends.

On a computer, the up arrow goes high and the down arrow goes low. Space hops to the other road. P or Esc pauses.
