## Long description

Thousands of years after a world war, people live again at a village scale: stone houses, horses, carts, and donkeys. There are no cars and no machines in daily life.

You are Azad. Part 1 is a dream. Neighbours talk to each other. You overhear them. You gather, craft, and keep fragments of a lost civilization in a paper diary. At the star spring a red seed and a white stone are a choice. The red seed wakes you into the real, harsher Sharsten.

Play is on foot. You can look, crouch, jump, climb a low roof, and, past the village edge, face imaginary thornbeasts. There is no blood.
