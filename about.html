About Happy Toons Studio

A small creative studio making bright, gentle cartoons and games for kids.

What we do

Happy Toons Studio makes custom 3D cartoons that star your child, with gentle lessons in kindness, sharing, listening, helping at home and love for family. We also make games, like ToonyTaps for little learners and the browser games Toonblade and خەلیفەکان.

On our YouTube channel, Ellie the baby elephant and her friends Bella, Finn, Dotty, Pip, Flip and Niko live in Happy Town.

Three languages

We work in English, Kurdish (Sorani) and Arabic, so more families can enjoy stories in their own language.

How we work

  • AI-assisted, human-checked. We use AI tools, and a human writes, edits and checks every piece of work.
  • Original characters. No copyrighted characters.
  • Kid-safe. No ads or tracking on this website, and we ask for as little information as possible. See Privacy & Children.
  • Honest. No fake reviews, fake followers or invented numbers.
