About Happy Toons Studio

An independent UK studio for 3D animation, games, video and creative digital services.

What we do

Happy Toons Studio is a small, independent studio run from the United Kingdom. We work across four areas:

  • 3D animation and video. Short animated pieces, promo videos for products and events, and short-form video for social platforms.
  • Games. Browser games such as Toonblade, خەلیفەکان (Khalifakan) and Block Tower, plus a Windows demo, Gurut, on itch.io.
  • Creative services. One-page websites, logos and social graphics, voiceover and subtitles, translation and copywriting. See Services.
  • YouTube channels. StepWise Simple (step-by-step phone fixes) and Coilwise (quick science and home-electrics shorts).

Our family work, including custom cartoons and the Happy Town series, now lives in its own Kids & Family section.

Languages

We work in English, Kurdish (Sorani) and Arabic, for clients and audiences in all three.

How we work

  • AI-assisted, human-reviewed. We use AI tools in production. A person writes, edits and checks every deliverable, and we tell clients which parts were AI-assisted.
  • Original work. Original characters and assets only. No copyrighted characters, and no real people's voices without permission.
  • Clear pricing. We confirm the scope and price in writing before any payment.
  • Honest marketing. No fake reviews, bought followers or invented numbers.
  • Privacy. No ads or cookies on this website, only anonymous visit counts. See Privacy.
