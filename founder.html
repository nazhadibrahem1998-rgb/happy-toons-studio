Early work and Happy Toons Studio

Ayub Azizi founded Happy Toons Studio and runs it from the UK. The studio started with custom 3D cartoon videos for kids, offered through the Fiverr profile ayubazizi_art, and its first website was published on GitHub Pages before moving to happytoonsstudio.com.

Alongside cartoons and games, the studio offers creative services such as one-page websites, logos and posters, promo videos, AI voiceovers and subtitles, and translation between Kurdish (Sorani), Arabic and English.

Cartoons

The studio's main product is a custom 3D cartoon starring a child, with their name, age and favourite things, and a gentle lesson in kindness, sharing, listening or helping at home. Stories can be made in English, Kurdish (Sorani) or Arabic, with original, child-safe characters.

On the Happy Toons Studio YouTube channel, Ellie the baby elephant and her friends Bella, Finn, Dotty, Pip, Flip and Niko live in Happy Town, in colourful cartoons for little kids about colours, numbers, ABC, animals and good habits.

Games

  • Toonblade: a free 3D cartoon arena game that runs in the browser on computers and phones. The hero, Lang, fights waves of goblins, skeletons and brutes, with a Skeleton King boss. No downloads, accounts or ads. itch.io page · play here.
  • Gurut (گڕوت), free demo: a third-person shooter demo for Windows, made with the Godot engine and tagged Kurdish, in which the player clears a mountain village of the fictional Iron Empire. It is listed as in development. itch.io page.
  • خەلیفەکان (Khalifakan): a 3D adventure game in Kurdish (Sorani) that plays in the browser and can be added to a phone's home screen. Play here.
  • ToonyTaps: ABC & 123 for Kids: an Android learning app with letters, numbers, colours and simple maths, currently in closed testing on Google Play. More.
  • A new game is in development.

Toonblade and Khalifakan include cartoon sword combat, so they are best for older kids. All games are listed on HappyToonsStudio on itch.io.

Approach to AI-assisted work

🤖 + ✍️ Honest note: the studio's cartoons and art are AI-assisted. AI tools help with images, video, voice, text and code, and a human writes, edits and checks the work before it is shared.

  • The studio's games on itch.io carry an AI disclosure, and the StepWise Simple channel says its videos are AI-assisted (script and voice).
  • Original characters only, with no copyrighted characters and no real people's voices without permission.
  • No fake reviews, fake followers or invented numbers.
  • This website has no ads, no tracking and no cookies. See Privacy & Children.
