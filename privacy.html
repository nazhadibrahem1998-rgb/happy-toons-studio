🔒 Privacy & Children

Privacy & Children

Last updated: 31 October 2026

In short: the studio pages have no sign-up, no ads, no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. Progress in our browser games stays on your device. بورجی خشت (Block Tower), a game for teens, has an optional anonymous feedback note. What that note collects is explained on its privacy page.

This website

  • We do not use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking scripts.
  • There are no accounts and no chat. The studio pages have no forms. Block Tower's optional feedback note is described on its own privacy page.
  • Fonts and images are hosted with the site, so loading pages does not send your data to font or ad companies.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may process technical data such as IP addresses to deliver and secure the site. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Children (COPPA and UK Children's Code)

Our cartoons and games are made for kids, and we take children's privacy seriously.

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under the age required in your country).
  • Kids should not contact us directly. A parent or carer should get in touch.
  • If you think a child has sent us personal information, email hello@happytoonsstudio.com and we will delete it.

Our games

  • Toonblade, خەلیفەکان and بورجی خشت (Block Tower) run in your browser with no account. They may save things like your best score, settings or, for Block Tower, a feedback note waiting on your device. This stays on your device and is not sent to us unless you choose to send Block Tower feedback after that feature is switched on. You can clear it in your browser settings. Details: Block Tower privacy.
  • ToonyTaps is an Android app, currently in testing. Its privacy details will be shown on its Google Play listing when it is released.

Custom cartoon orders

Orders are placed by parents or adults through Fiverr or by email. We only ask for what the story needs: a first name, age and a few favourite things. Please don't send photos, surnames, addresses or school names. We use these details only to make your cartoon, and delete them on request.

Fiverr handles payment and its own data under Fiverr's Privacy Policy.

Links to other sites

We link to YouTube, TikTok, Fiverr and itch.io. These sites have their own privacy policies and age rules. Many of them are not meant for children under 13 without a parent.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email hello@happytoonsstudio.com.
