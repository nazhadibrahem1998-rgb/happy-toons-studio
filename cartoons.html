FAQ

Questions parents ask

Is the video made with AI?

Yes. Scenes are made with AI-assisted tools, and every video is written, edited and checked by a human before delivery.

What lessons can the story teach?

Kindness, sharing, listening, helping at home, saying thank you, loving Mom and family, plus colours, numbers and manners.

Will it look like my child?

Your child becomes a cute cartoon character inspired by your description, with their name. It is not a copy of a photo, so no photo is needed.

What information do you need?

Only what the story needs, given by a parent: a first name, age and a few favourite things. Please don't send photos, surnames, addresses or school names. See Privacy & Children.

Can I share it?

Yes, with family and friends and on your own social accounts.
