No links in this section. Paste as-is.

### English

**Title:** Toonblade

**Short description:** Play as Lang. Survive goblin waves, dodge the archers, and defeat the Skeleton King.

**Description:**

Toonblade is a cartoon sword arena from Happy Toons Studio. You play as Lang.

Goblins and skeletons rush the ring. Goblin archers hang back and shoot, so keep moving. Every 10th wave the Skeleton King steps in. Block his slam, dodge the sweep, and strike when the opening is there.

Clear a wave to catch your breath and heal a little. Potions drop from tougher enemies. How long can Lang last?

Made with AI-assisted tools.

**Controls:**

Desktop: WASD or the arrow keys move. The mouse looks around. Q and E turn the camera too. Click or J attacks. Right-click or K blocks. Space dodges. P or Esc pauses. M mutes the sound.

Phone and tablet: the joystick on the left moves Lang. Drag on the right side of the screen to look. The sword button attacks. The shield button blocks. The curved-arrow button dodges. Pause and sound sit at the top.

**Suggested category:** Action

**Suggested tags:** sword, arena, cartoon, boss, survival, fantasy
