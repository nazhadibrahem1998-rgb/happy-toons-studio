Happy Toons Studio is an independent creative studio in the UK working in 3D animation, browser games, short-form video and digital services such as websites, branding and translation. It works in English, Kurdish (Sorani) and Arabic, and keeps its family content in a separate Kids & Family section.

Early work and Happy Toons Studio

Happy Toons Studio is run from the UK. The studio started with custom 3D cartoon videos, offered through the Fiverr profile ayubazizi_art, and its first website was published on GitHub Pages before moving to happytoonsstudio.com.

Alongside animation and games, the studio offers creative services such as one-page websites, logos and posters, promo videos, AI voiceovers and subtitles, and translation between Kurdish (Sorani), Arabic and English.

Animation and family work

The studio makes short 3D animation and promo video for clients. For families it offers a custom 3D cartoon featuring a child's name, age and favourite things, in English, Kurdish (Sorani) or Arabic, with original characters.

On the Happy Toons Studio YouTube channel, Ellie the baby elephant and her friends Bella, Finn, Dotty, Pip, Flip and Niko live in Happy Town, in cartoons for young children about colours, numbers, letters, animals and good habits.

Games

  • Toonblade: a free 3D arena action game that runs in the browser on computers and phones. The hero, Lang, fights waves of goblins, skeletons and brutes, with a Skeleton King boss. No downloads, accounts or ads. itch.io page · play here.
  • Gurut (گڕوت), free demo: a third-person shooter demo for Windows, made with the Godot engine and tagged Kurdish, in which the player clears a mountain village of the fictional Iron Empire. It is listed as in development. itch.io page.
  • خەلیفەکان (Khalifakan): a 3D adventure game in Kurdish (Sorani) that plays in the browser and can be added to a phone's home screen. Play here.
  • بورجی خشت (Block Tower): a night-time 3D tower climb that plays in the browser. Play here.
  • ToonyTaps: ABC & 123 for Kids: an Android learning app for young children with letters, numbers, colours and simple maths, currently in closed testing on Google Play. More.
  • A new game is in development.

Toonblade and Khalifakan include stylised sword combat and suit teens and adults. All games are listed on HappyToonsStudio on itch.io.

Approach to AI-assisted work

Note: the studio's cartoons and art are AI-assisted. AI tools help with images, video, voice, text and code, and a human writes, edits and checks the work before it is shared.

  • The studio's games on itch.io carry an AI disclosure, and the StepWise Simple channel says its videos are AI-assisted (script and voice).
  • Original characters only, with no copyrighted characters and no real people's voices without permission.
  • No fake reviews, fake followers or invented numbers.
  • This website has no ads and no cookies, and only counts visits anonymously. See Privacy & Children.
