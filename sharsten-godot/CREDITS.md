## Poly Haven

- Files: `assets/textures/polyhaven/ground.jpg` (aerial_grass_rock), `plaster.jpg` (plastered_wall), `wood.jpg` (wood_floor), `rock.jpg` (rock_boulder_dry), `mud.jpg` (brown_mud_dry), `rock_face.jpg` (aerial_rocks_02)
- Used for: grassland, plaster, timber, stone walls, and the distant ridges.
- Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/aerial_grass_rock https://polyhaven.com/a/plastered_wall https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_floor https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_boulder_dry https://polyhaven.com/a/brown_mud_dry https://polyhaven.com/a/aerial_rocks_02
- Licence: CC0 1.0 — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The dream sky is an original procedural golden-hour sky. A midday Poly Haven HDRI was not used, because it would wash out that light.

The Newroz hearth music is an original eight-second loop written for this game: a daf pulse and a short melody. It is not a recording, and it is not Hasan Zirak's Newroz song or any other published tune. The fire crackle, wind, and door creak are original waveforms in `scripts/world/ambience.gd` and `scripts/world/house_door.gd`.

Wooden house doors, the short creak, interior mats, hearths, shelves, jars, and low tables are original geometry. The creak is synthesized in `scripts/world/house_door.gd`. It is not a downloaded sample.

## This project

Doorway rugs, wall hangings, cloth weaves, the black-grey-cream jamane, and the noise ridges are original. Roads are packed earth and cobble. No kilim is used as a road.
