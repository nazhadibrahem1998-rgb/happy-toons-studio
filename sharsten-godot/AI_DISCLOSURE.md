# AI disclosure

Sharsten was made with an AI coding assistant. The assistant wrote and revised game code, English text, the procedural audio generator, and the art direction for the original low-poly village.

No AI image model, AI music model, or AI voice model was used to generate a shipped asset. Owner-supplied concept frames were used as art direction only and are not included in the game. Textures on the ground, walls, timber, mud, and distant rock are Poly Haven CC0 photographs. Doorway rugs, wall hangings, wool, cotton, embroidery, and black-grey-cream jamane checks are original images made in code. Roads are not carpeted. The wind, birds, tandoor crackle, UI clicks, and the quiet theme are original waveforms synthesized in `scripts/world/ambience.gd`. Bodies and the idle, walk, talk, and sit clips are the Quaternius Universal Animation Library (CC0). Kurdish clothing and hair are original geometry parented to that skeleton. Houses use Kenney Fantasy Town Kit pieces (CC0). Trees and bushes are the Quaternius Ultimate Nature pack (CC0). See `CREDITS.md`.

Azad-i Asmani and the villagers are fictional. No real person is depicted. The game names no political party, flag, symbol, slogan, or leader, and it does not retell any real conflict.

This file is the store disclosure. The in-game credits say the same thing in short.
