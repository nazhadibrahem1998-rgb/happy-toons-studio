ToonyTaps: ABC & 123 for Kids

A free learning app for toddlers and preschoolers aged 2 to 5. Children meet Pip and tap big, bright cards to hear letters, numbers, colours and animal sounds out loud. No ads, no account, and it works fully offline.

What's inside

Tap, look and listen with Pip

Every card talks: tap it and Pip says the word out loud, so little ones learn by looking, listening and tapping.

ABC

All 26 letters, big and small, each with a picture word: A is for Apple, B is for Bear, C is for Cat…

123

Count from 1 to 20 with fun pictures to count along.

Colours

Red, blue, yellow, green and more, each with a colourful example.

Animals

16 animals and the sounds they make: the cow says moo, the duck says quack.

For parents

Simple, offline and private

  • ✓Big colourful buttons made for small fingers.
  • ✓Clear spoken words using your phone's built-in voice.
  • ✓Stars and cheers to keep children motivated.
  • ✓Works fully offline — useful for car trips and flights.
  • ✓No ads, no in-app purchases and no account.

Free on Google Play, for ages 2 to 5. Content rating: PEGI 3. First public release, updated 22 November 2026. If no voice is heard, check that a text-to-speech voice is installed in the phone's settings (Settings > Accessibility > Text-to-speech) and that the volume is up.
